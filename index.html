🎨 Manga Colorizer

Bring your favorite manga to life with a splash of color directly in your browser.

How it Works

The Manga Colorizer is a simple bookmarklet. It applies advanced CSS filters to images on manga reading sites to simulate a full-color release.

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Manga Colorizer

Click the bookmarklet button above to test it on this page!

Installation Instructions

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (usually Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Click and drag the green Manga Colorizer button to your bookmarks bar.
  3. Go to your favorite manga reading website.
  4. Click the Manga Colorizer bookmark.
  5. Enjoy the vibrant colors!
